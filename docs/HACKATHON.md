## One-liner
ReelTale is a WebNovel-style home for serialized short video stories. You can watch creators' live-action and
stop-motion series, or write your own opening and let Meta's models turn it into a branching, illustrated,
narrated story you steer by voice.

## The problem (Build for Your World)
_[2–3 sentences: short-drama apps are closed studios. Webnovel proved serialized UGC + coins works for text. Making video
is expensive. Meta's models make illustrated, branching video-like stories cheap enough for anyone.]_

| Muse Wonder | Plans the whole branching arc at story start; writes the "AI editor's report" at publish | Progress screen; Publish screen; editor queue |

### 3-minute walkthrough
1. Home → **Try the demo story** → tap a choice, then press the mic and *say* the next choice.
2. Tap ⓘ **How this scene was made** → toggle **Show SAM mask**.
3. Create → **Write an AI story** → dictate an opening → watch Muse Wonder plan it.
4. Open the series "…" → episode 4 is locked → unlock with coins → captions are generated by Muse Voice.
5. Publish your story → read the AI editor's report → note the AI label and EU icon.

## Production readiness (why this can launch in November)
- 18+ gate enforced in the database, as Meta's terms require; per-purpose AI consent naming the providers.
- Double-entry-style coin ledger; sandbox purchases can't create real money; idempotent webhooks.
- Moderation before publishing, reporting on every scene, EU DSA notice form, AI labels + C2PA provenance.
- Cost control: every model call is reserved against per-job and global daily budgets before it is made.
